Repair recent prices before auditing

The failed audit found 1,079 missing daily prices out of 5,488. Its reconciliation step only queued repairs; the next step checked coverage immediately. Regular repair jobs selected at most 100 targets from a backlog exceeding 91,000.

New behavior

  1. Reconcile missing targets.
  2. Repair due targets from the last seven closed UTC days, bounded to 40 minutes and 10,000 claims.
  3. Check finalized coverage, including after a repair-step failure. Remaining gaps still fail the audit.

Regular repair jobs also select the recent window ahead of older backlog. Existing retry cooldowns, row locks, leases, batching and provider limits remain in force.

Validation and limits

TypeScript typecheck passed. Tests and lint were not run locally, per the project instruction for Astra. This change does not guarantee full coverage: unresolved feeds and retries can leave gaps, and older backlog is processed when no recent due work occupies the claim limit.

The workflow change takes effect after merge. This document does not claim that the production backlog has been repaired.
