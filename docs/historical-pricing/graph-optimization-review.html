Correctness

Every graph rebuilds its selected dependency proof and validates historical evidence. Cache keys separate chains, dates, explicit blocks and cancellation contexts. Rejected cache entries are evicted. Cycle/depth failures and selected derived paths are not cached as reusable results. All session caches are bounded.

Validation

Typechecking passed locally. Regression coverage checks shared block reads without client/address leakage, transient recovery, cache bounds, shared nested dependencies, cutoff recovery, historical isolation and wider graph frontiers without increased concurrency. Tests and lint run in CI, not locally under the Astra instruction. No end-to-end production speedup is claimed before measurement.

Better pricing logic?

Keep the dependency graph, with alternative valuation recipes anchored in independent historical prices. A vault share may be priced from its share conversion and asset value even without a direct quote. Better coverage requires verified anchors and adapters, plus demand-driven selection of credible routes. A cycle of ratios cannot determine an absolute USD price.
