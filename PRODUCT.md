# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Yearn operators and engineers running, validating, and troubleshooting the canonical daily price process. They need to understand a long-running queue from desktop or mobile without querying Postgres or parsing logs.

## Product Purpose

Yearn Prices maintains one accepted historical price per asset and closed UTC calendar day. The operator surface makes durable queue state, accepted evidence, unsupported assets, retries, quarantines, throughput, sources, adapters, and quality visible without collapsing those outcomes into a misleading coverage number.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, and operational. The interface should feel trustworthy during routine monitoring and useful during investigations.

## Anti-references

Avoid trading-terminal theatrics, crypto-neon styling, promotional metrics, decorative charting, and dashboards that hide status definitions behind color or vague labels.

## Design Principles

1. Preserve the meaning of every durable state and distinguish terminal outcomes from accepted prices.
2. Lead with the operational questions: is it running, how far is it, and what needs attention?
3. Prefer exact timestamps, auditable records, and explicit definitions over decorative summaries.
4. Protect credentials and provider URLs while retaining actionable failure detail.
5. Keep the monitoring surface usable on desktop and narrow screens.

## Accessibility

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard operation, visible focus, reduced motion, high contrast in light and dark environments, semantic tables and forms, live-region updates, and status cues that do not rely on color alone.
